# Revnu — Your AI Growth Hire

> Revnu is an AI growth team for startups. Connect your product and agents start finding customers, testing ideas, and learning across channels.

Canonical: https://revnu.com

Revnu is your AI growth hire — the growth team for startups. AI made coding
easier than ever, which means the product is not the moat anymore —
distribution is. Revnu does all the boring growth work technical founders know
they should do but never get round to. You stay in the codebase. Revnu handles
the rest.

## What Revnu does

- Finds and qualifies leads from real signals
- Drafts cold outbound, partnership pitches, and reporter intros
- Writes SEO content in your voice for Google and AI search
- Runs ad experiments across Meta, LinkedIn, Reddit, TikTok, and other channels
- Turns product wins into short-form video and social posts
- Tests pricing, landing-page copy, CTAs, and onboarding flows
- Sends approvals and weekly recaps through iMessage, Slack, and email
